## 2026-08-30 Multi-scope provenance native executability blocker

The macOS obligation for `SCN-PERF-MULTI-SCOPE-PROVENANCE-001` passes every
client call assertion, every rebuild identity, and the whole server state
projection once the registry generation fact tracks activations.

Two authored facts do not reconcile.

- `rebuild_count` is 6 authored and 9 observed.
- `client-a` is authored with `row_count` 0, `provenance_count` 0,
  `checkpoint_count` 0, and `rebuild_attempt_count` 0. The observed client
  reports 1 for each.

Both differences have one cause. The scenario authors `connect/send` for
client-a, client-b, and client-c and authors no pull and no rebuild for them.
The reference model therefore records a connect only, and those clients stay
inert. The authored client facts state exactly that.

The native client cannot perform a connect alone. `SynchroClient` exposes
`start`, `syncNow`, `retryAfterError`, and `resetSchemaAndStart`. The client
contract defines connect as step 2 of the sync loop, not an independent
operation. Driving the authored connect therefore also rebuilds and pulls, so
each of those clients gains one rebuild session, one row, one provenance
record, and one checkpoint.

Evidence that the extra work is protocol-required. Every client in
`model.setup[0].payload.clients` declares `last_cursor_acknowledged_at: null`,
and the wire protocol requires a rebuild for an assigned scope with no valid
receipt. The scenario commits `scope-topology-row-000001` into `scope-a` at
step 4, before client-a connects at step 6.

A tested and rejected resolution. Establishing client-a, client-b, and
client-c with a start call before their authored call, and attributing those
sessions to setup, made `rebuild_count` reach 6. The authored client facts
rejected the result because those clients then reported one rebuild attempt
and one row each. The change was reverted in `c34fa67`.

Consequence. The scenario is not executable at the native layer as authored.
Resolving it requires a user decision on one of these.

- Re-author the scenario so client-a, client-b, and client-c perform a full
  synchronization, and state the resulting client facts and rebuild count.
- Re-author the scenario so those clients are absent from the native cells.
- Add a connect-only client operation, which the client contract does not
  define today.
