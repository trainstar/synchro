### 2026-09-08: R3.2 And R3.3 Complete

The live soak passed on the desktop against real PostgreSQL at commit `74e67ac`.

- Mutations carried a UUID `client_version` instead of the canonical microsecond timestamp `extensions/synchro-core/src/contract.rs:782` requires.
- Engine defect: `mutation-conservation` required a UUID `client_version`, contradicting the contract. The rule was authored against a wrong assumption and passed every fixture review. Only real bytes exposed it. The rule and its fixtures now follow the contract.
- `ConfigForDuration` modeled 16 operations per second, which is unrunnable for live operations that wait on WAL materialization and process restarts.
